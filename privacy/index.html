Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 26, 2026

Features and optional services can differ by app version and testing programme. The sections below apply when the relevant feature is available and used.

This Privacy Policy explains how Chess Opening Trainer handles data. The app is developed and published by Quackline Studio.

1. Who we are

Quackline Studio is the developer of Chess Opening Trainer. For privacy questions, contact quackline.studio@proton.me.

2. Privacy summary

  • No separate Chess Opening Trainer account is required. A chess-service username or purchasing Google Account is managed by the relevant external service, not an app-created account.
  • The app does not upload your repertoire, training history or imported games to Quackline Studio. Optional purchase verification sends separate billing information, as described in section 8a.
  • The app has no advertising or third-party usage-analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Training reminders are local. Restricted billing-operation logs and server-to-server events are described below; they are not phone push notifications.
  • The app stores chess and training data locally. Optional online lookups, voluntary support email, exported files and operating-system backups have the separate data flows described below.
  • At first use, the app stores only the selected player age range. Profiles for players under 13 cannot access online chess services, feedback email, or Google Play rating actions.
  • Chess.com and Lichess usernames are sent to the selected service when you request a game import and, if automatic refresh is enabled, when checking for new games on the Games screen.
  • Optional online position analysis sends the current chess position to Lichess after your consent. Offline Stockfish analysis sends nothing over the network.
  • The optional feedback action opens your chosen email app. Nothing is sent until you review and send the email.
  • If you choose to submit a rating or review, it is sent directly to Google Play and handled under Google's policies.

3. Data stored on your device

Chess Opening Trainer may store the following data in the app's private local storage:

  • opening-book names, chess positions, moves, variations, and spaced-repetition state;
  • up to 2,000 saved engine evaluations, including position, score, depth, variations, source and timestamp, and your online-analysis consent;
  • training preferences, selected repertoires, language, theme, notation, onboarding status, and the selected player age range;
  • whether the optional daily training reminder is enabled and the time you selected;
  • successful-Sprint counters and review-request timestamps used to limit how often the native Google Play review prompt may be requested;
  • the Chess.com and Lichess usernames you enter when using a profile for a player aged 13 or older;
  • opening-book data you import in Chess Opening Trainer format.

Opening books you export are written to a location you choose and are then under your control. Game data downloaded from Chess.com or Lichess, including usernames, game identifiers, results, timestamps and PGN moves, is saved in the app's private local storage for offline access after restart. Local refresh records help retrieve missing games without importing duplicates. These records are not uploaded to Quackline Studio.

Your device or account settings may enable operating-system backup or device-to-device transfer of app data. These copies are controlled by the operating system and its provider, not a Quackline Studio chess-data backup service. Deleting local app data does not itself remove exported files or OS-managed backup copies; manage those through their own settings.

Local weekly repertoire review: The review compares games from the selected imported account with selected repertoire books on your device. When you save a reply and start follow-up, the private weekly_review_v1 directory stores the account/book scope, chess positions, move paths, game identifiers, observation times and the replies recorded at that time. This feature does not upload these checkpoints or introduce a subscription purchase. A failed checkpoint save may remain in memory for retry until the app closes; the saved book reply is separate.

The same private storage remembers the most recently selected imported account and books, including an intentionally empty book selection. Restarting follow-up clears both follow-up and this saved choice. Deleting imported games in Settings also clears both, while keeping books and training statistics. Selecting an under-13 profile clears these records and prevents access to the game-based weekly review. Selection saving and deletion errors are reported so they can be retried.

Personal daily practice: Your device can build a short plan from selected repertoire books and, optionally, already downloaded games. It counts repeated choices outside your saved repertoire; this is not a chess-engine judgment about move quality. Private daily-practice storage remembers the selected account/books, time budget, plan date, positions and saved replies, deviation counts, and completed or dismissed tasks so you can resume after reopening. It does not upload these records or store another copy of your PGN games. Actual practice answers update the existing local repetition state and aggregate training statistics.

Visible daily history is limited to 64 plans and 30 days, checked when the storage is loaded or written. Two committed recovery generations are kept; the preceding recovery copy may contain an expired plan until another successful save replaces it. You can delete the daily plans and selection from the daily screen. Deleting imported games in Settings or selecting an under-13 profile also clears daily records and invalidates pending saves, keeping your books and training statistics. The daily screen requires a configured 13+ profile. Saved own plans remain readable and deletable independently of the subscription entitlement. Read, save and deletion failures are reported for retry.

4. Chess.com and Lichess integrations

These online integrations are available only for player profiles aged 13 or older. When you enter a public username and tap the corresponding import button, the app sends that username over HTTPS directly to the selected service. It uses the public APIs and may supplement Chess.com results from its publicly accessible game list and game analysis pages, without logging in or using account cookies. The response may include public game information such as player usernames, ratings, game results, timestamps, game identifiers, URLs, and PGN move data.

After a successful import, automatic refresh can check the saved usernames when you open the Games screen and about once per minute while that screen is visible and the app is in the foreground. It is enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings under Automatically add new games. It does not start imports for names that have never been imported successfully. Leaving the Games screen or backgrounding the app pauses new refreshes; requests already in progress may finish. There is no background polling service or remote push service. Existing downloaded games remain stored when automatic refresh is turned off.

Manual imports and automatic refreshes are used only to display and compare public games with your local repertoire. The selected service also receives normal network information, such as your IP address and request metadata, and handles it under its own policies:

  • Chess.com Privacy Policy and User Agreement
  • Lichess Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

Chess Opening Trainer is not affiliated with or endorsed by Chess.com or Lichess. Their services, availability, and data practices are controlled by their respective operators.

Optional position analysis: Offline Stockfish runs entirely on your device. For profiles aged 13 or older, you may separately enable Lichess Cloud after an in-app disclosure. While online analysis is enabled on a visible screen, the current position in FEN format and analysis options are sent directly to Lichess over HTTPS. Lichess receives normal network metadata, including your IP address; no book name, username, full game or training history is attached. The service returns existing public cloud evaluations and does not start a new server calculation. Results may be saved locally and are not uploaded or synchronized by Quackline Studio. You can switch online analysis off at any time. Leaving the screen or backgrounding the app stops analysis.

5. Optional local training reminders

You may enable one daily training reminder and choose its time in Settings. The app requests notification permission only when you turn this feature on. The reminder is scheduled by your device's operating system; its enabled state and selected time are stored locally. No reminder data is sent to Quackline Studio or to a remote notification service.

You can change the time or disable the reminder at any time in Settings. You can also control notification permission in your device settings. Delivery may be delayed or prevented by operating-system, battery, or device-vendor restrictions.

6. Direct feedback email

For player profiles aged 13 or older, the Settings screen can open your chosen email app with a draft addressed to Quackline Studio. You decide whether to send it and may edit or delete any part of the draft first. If you send the email, Quackline Studio receives the message you wrote, your sender email address and normal email metadata, and any attachment you add yourself.

You may choose whether the draft includes diagnostic information. The optional diagnostic block contains only the app version and build number, platform, app language, and selected theme. Chess Opening Trainer does not attach opening books, positions, moves, games, usernames, training history, notification settings, or other repertoire data.

The email is sent by your selected external email app and provider under their own privacy and security practices. Chess Opening Trainer does not send the message in the background and does not learn whether you sent it. Support correspondence is used to respond to feedback, investigate reported issues, and improve the app.

7. Google Play ratings and reviews

For player profiles aged 13 or older, the Settings screen can open the app's Google Play listing so you can leave a rating or review. After several flawless completed Sprint training sessions, the app may also ask Google Play to display its native in-app review card. This request is rate-limited locally and is never shown after a failed or incomplete training session. No app feature or reward depends on leaving a review.

If you choose to submit a star rating or written review, Google Play receives and stores that content. A production review may be public; feedback from a closed testing track may be shared privately with the developer. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service. You can manage or delete a submitted review through your Google Play or Google Account.

Chess Opening Trainer does not receive the rating or review through a Quackline Studio server, and the in-app review API does not tell the app whether you submitted one. The app stores only local counters and timestamps needed to avoid requesting the review card too often.

7a. Android app updates

On Android, the app can ask Google Play whether an update is available when the app starts, including for under-13 profiles. Google documents that this process handles device metadata, the app version and which modules or asset packs of this app are installed, to determine update availability and size. Google states that the data is encrypted, is not transferred to third parties and is deleted after a fixed retention period; we do not have a verified number of days to state here. This platform check is separate from optional game imports, reviews and purchase verification. See Google's update data information and the Google Privacy Policy.

8. How data is used and shared

Local app data is used to provide repertoire editing, training, statistics, preferences, import/export, and game comparison. Quackline Studio does not sell personal data and does not share local app data with advertisers or data brokers.

The app sends public usernames and ordinary network metadata to Chess.com or Lichess for manual game imports and enabled automatic refreshes. The refresh setting and scheduling timestamps are stored only on your device. Optional online analysis also sends the current chess position and analysis options to Lichess while enabled. Downloaded games and locally calculated engine results are not sent to a Quackline Studio server.

Feedback is sent only when you choose to send the prepared draft from an external email app. Quackline Studio uses received support messages only for communication, troubleshooting, and product improvement.

8a. Optional Google Play subscriptions and verification

This section applies if you use a version that offers Google Play subscriptions. Availability can differ by version or testing programme. Buying is optional; the original free repertoire, import, editing, analysis, practice and statistics features do not require an app account or purchase.

For a configured player profile aged 13 or older, Google Play handles payment, the purchasing Google Account and Google's purchase records under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service. The app does not request your payment-card details or Google Account email address. For purchase verification, restoration or a status refresh, it sends the Play purchase token over HTTPS to the verification service operated by Quackline Studio at sakk.benedekbrandschott.com/billing. The token identifies a purchase; it is not a chess-service username. The service asks Google Play for the current subscription status, acknowledges eligible new purchases and returns access, expiry and renewal information.

Google Play sends subscription-change events through Google Cloud Pub/Sub to this service, including while the app is closed. These server-to-server events trigger another Google status check and are not notifications to your phone. Billing requests and events do not carry opening books, FEN positions, PGN moves, imported games, chess-service usernames, weekly/daily checkpoints or training history. The optional chess-service lookups in section 4 have their own data flows.

The verification service runs on Hetzner infrastructure. It processes raw purchase tokens and request data in memory, without its own purchase database or persistent access cache. Our normal Pub/Sub delivery subscription can retain unacknowledged token-bearing event messages for up to 24 hours while retrying delivery; it does not retain acknowledged messages. This Cloud queue is separate from the verifier's memory.

To check operation and diagnose delivery failures, the server stores restricted event logs: processing timestamp, a hash of the Pub/Sub message ID, event type, outcome and HTTP status; after successful verification, subscription state, expiry, verification time, acknowledgement and auto-renewal flags. The hash comes from an event ID, not a purchase token, and we do not claim that these records are necessarily anonymous. This application audit excludes raw tokens, request bodies, authorization headers, Google error bodies, order IDs, email addresses and client IP addresses. Its Docker logs rotate at 1 MiB with two files; this is a size limit, not a guaranteed retention period in days.

The reverse proxy processes normal connection metadata and can produce operational/error logs. No access log is enabled for the relevant service, but its separate operational output is stored by Docker and has no configured container-level rotation or verified time-based deletion limit. Hosting/network providers and their backups can have separate retention practices; no fixed provider retention or hosting-region guarantee is stated here. Request limiting uses socket IP and counters in memory, cleared on a later request in a new time window or process restart, without a guaranteed idle-time deletion interval. We do not use this data to determine your location or build an advertising profile.

The app holds its verified access status in memory and checks it again online after reopening or returning to the app and periodically during use. Active Pro after an offline restart is not guaranteed. Google maintains its own purchase records. Switching to an under-13 or unconfigured profile blocks new client billing actions and Pro access, but it does not cancel an existing Google subscription or stop Google's server-side billing events. Uninstalling or deleting local data also does not stop recurring charges. Manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play subscriptions.

9. Retention and data deletion requests

Local data, including the selected age range and review-request counters, remains on your device until you delete the relevant data, clear the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstall the app. Downloaded games and their refresh records can be deleted separately in Settings; this does not delete opening books or training statistics. Quackline Studio has no server-side copy of this local app data.

Feedback emails may remain in the Quackline Studio support mailbox for as long as reasonably needed to answer the message, investigate the issue, maintain support records, or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by writing to the contact address below.

Requests concerning data held by Chess.com or Lichess must be directed to that service using the controls and contact methods described in its privacy policy.

Request deletion: email quackline.studio@proton.me with the subject “Chess Opening Trainer data deletion”. You may ask us to delete support correspondence and developer-held operational data that can be linked to you. Describe the relevant message or event and approximate date; you do not need to provide a raw purchase token. We will review which records can be identified and removed, and explain any technical or legitimate legal/security retention limits. There is no Chess Opening Trainer account to close. Local-data deletion does not delete Google purchase records or cancel a subscription; use Google Play subscriptions separately. Our restricted audit and proxy/provider retention limits are explained in section 8a.

The analysis cache keeps at most 2,000 positions; the oldest updated entries are removed when this limit is reached. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app also removes the cache. Resetting training statistics does not clear analysis results.

10. Security

The app stores persistent data in its private application storage and uses HTTPS for chess-service requests and purchase verification. Feedback delivery is handled by your selected email app and provider. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so you should protect access to your device and keep backups of exported opening books where appropriate.

11. Children

The app asks the user to select a player age range before first use. It does not request or store a date of birth, name, contact detail, or proof of age. The selected range is stored in app-local preferences, is not attached to the billing or chess-service requests described here, and is used to provide an age-appropriate feature set. Operating-system backups are described in section 3.

For player profiles under 13, the app disables Chess.com and Lichess game import, Lichess Study import, direct feedback email, the Google Play rating link, and automatic in-app review requests. Selecting an under-13 profile also removes previously saved Chess.com and Lichess usernames, downloaded games and their refresh records. Local opening books, PGN import, training, statistics, and local reminders remain available.

The app does not create user accounts, contain advertising, or use analytics, crash-reporting, social, or remote push-notification SDKs. A parent or guardian should review the app and these documents when required by local law.

Under-13 profiles cannot initiate purchase, restoration or verification in the app. This does not cancel a previously held Google Play subscription. The Android update check described in section 7a can still run. Under-13 profiles cannot use Lichess Cloud analysis. Offline Stockfish analysis and previously saved local evaluations remain available without a network request.
