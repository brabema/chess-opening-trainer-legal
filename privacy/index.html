Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chess Opening Trainer handles data. The app is developed and published by Quackline Studio.

1. Who we are

Quackline Studio is the developer of Chess Opening Trainer. For privacy questions, contact quackline.studio@proton.me.

2. Privacy summary

  • The app does not require an account.
  • Quackline Studio does not operate an app backend and does not receive your repertoires, training activity, usernames, or imported games.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or remote push-notification service. Optional training reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Your app data is stored locally on your device unless you explicitly export a file.
  • Chess.com and Lichess usernames are sent to the selected service only when you request a game import.
  • If you choose to submit a rating or review, it is sent directly to Google Play and handled under Google's policies.

3. Data stored on your device

Chess Opening Trainer may store the following data in the app's private local storage:

  • opening-book names, chess positions, moves, variations, and spaced-repetition state;
  • training preferences, selected repertoires, language, theme, notation, and onboarding status;
  • whether the optional daily training reminder is enabled and the time you selected;
  • successful-Sprint counters and review-request timestamps used to limit how often the native Google Play review prompt may be requested;
  • the Chess.com and Lichess usernames you enter;
  • opening-book data you import in Chess Opening Trainer format.

Opening books you export are written to a location you choose and are then under your control. Game data downloaded from Chess.com or Lichess is used for on-device analysis and held in app memory for the current use of the Games feature; it is not uploaded to Quackline Studio.

4. Chess.com and Lichess integrations

When you enter a public username and tap the corresponding import button, the app sends that username over HTTPS directly to the selected service's public API. The response may include public game information such as player usernames, ratings, game results, timestamps, game identifiers, URLs, and PGN move data.

These requests are user-initiated and are used only to display and compare public games with your local repertoire. The selected service also receives normal network information, such as your IP address and request metadata, and handles it under its own policies:

  • Chess.com Privacy Policy and User Agreement
  • Lichess Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

Chess Opening Trainer is not affiliated with or endorsed by Chess.com or Lichess. Their services, availability, and data practices are controlled by their respective operators.

5. Optional local training reminders

You may enable one daily training reminder and choose its time in Settings. The app requests notification permission only when you turn this feature on. The reminder is scheduled by your device's operating system; its enabled state and selected time are stored locally. No reminder data is sent to Quackline Studio or to a remote notification service.

You can change the time or disable the reminder at any time in Settings. You can also control notification permission in your device settings. Delivery may be delayed or prevented by operating-system, battery, or device-vendor restrictions.

6. Google Play ratings and reviews

The Settings screen can open the app's Google Play listing so you can leave a rating or review. After several flawless completed Sprint training sessions, the app may also ask Google Play to display its native in-app review card. This request is rate-limited locally and is never shown after a failed or incomplete training session. No app feature or reward depends on leaving a review.

If you choose to submit a star rating or written review, Google Play receives and stores that content. A production review may be public; feedback from a closed testing track may be shared privately with the developer. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service. You can manage or delete a submitted review through your Google Play or Google Account.

Chess Opening Trainer does not receive the rating or review through a Quackline Studio server, and the in-app review API does not tell the app whether you submitted one. The app stores only local counters and timestamps needed to avoid requesting the review card too often.

7. How data is used and shared

Local app data is used to provide repertoire editing, training, statistics, preferences, import/export, and game comparison. Quackline Studio does not sell personal data and does not share local app data with advertisers or data brokers.

The only third-party transfer performed by the current app is the user-requested transmission of a public username and ordinary network metadata to Chess.com or Lichess to retrieve public games. The downloaded game data remains on the device and is not sent to a Quackline Studio server.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data, including review-request counters, remains on your device until you delete the relevant opening book, clear the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstall the app. Session-only game results are discarded when they are no longer held by the running app. Quackline Studio has no server-side copy to delete.

Requests concerning data held by Chess.com or Lichess must be directed to that service using the controls and contact methods described in its privacy policy.

9. Security

The app stores persistent data in its private application storage and uses HTTPS for Chess.com and Lichess API requests. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so you should protect access to your device and keep backups of exported opening books where appropriate.

10. Children

The app does not create user accounts and does not knowingly collect personal data for Quackline Studio. A parent or guardian should supervise a minor's use of third-party chess services and ensure that it complies with those services' age requirements and terms.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy before introducing a material change to data handling, including analytics, crash reporting, advertising, cloud storage, or remote notification services. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version. Related Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated at the same time.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy or Chess Opening Trainer's data handling, email quackline.studio@proton.me.
